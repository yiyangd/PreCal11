原文差异与数学复审Source nuances and mathematical review

  • 8.1原书为两页概览，没有编号例题或习题；下一页Getting Ready归8.2。不添加虚构原题。Section 8.1 is a two-page overview without numbered examples or exercises. The next-page Getting Ready belongs to 8.2. No source questions are invented.
  • 8.2第94题把y=4px²与焦点(0,p)、准线y=−p同时列出，一般不相容。保留原式，明确区分正确的x²=4py及其条件证明。8.2#94 combines y=4 px² with focus(0,p) and directrix y=−p; these are generally inconsistent. The original remains, with the corrected x²=4 py reading and its conditional proof distinguished.
  • 8.2第101题标题为抛物线互动，但(a)(b)原文是指数增长/衰减文字；原链接返回404，不按标题猜测缺失活动。8.2#101 is headed as a parabola activity, but its printed parts discuss exponential growth/decay. Its link returns 404, so the unavailable activity is not inferred from the heading.
  • 8.3第61、62题配方后是正平方和=−1，实点集为空；第83题的给定点距原点超过半长轴，条件矛盾。8.3#61 and#62 complete to a positive sum of squares equal to−1, giving empty real loci. In#83 the given point lies farther from the origin than the semimajor axis, so the conditions are inconsistent.
  • 8.2第93题及8.3第100题的斜截式需非零分母，竖直切线另列。8.3第100题还印错常数项并误指10.2，网页保留原图并证明正确隐式切线。The slope-intercept tangent forms in 8.2#93 and 8.3#100 require nonzero denominators; vertical tangents are handled separately.8.3#100 also has an incorrect constant term and a misplaced 10.2 reference. Its source crop remains, with the correct implicit tangent proved.
  • 8.3第114题：轴平行坐标轴的教材范围内有8个椭圆；若允许任意旋转则有无穷多个。两种解释明确区分。For 8.3#114 there are eight ellipses under the section’s axis-parallel convention. Allowing arbitrary rotations gives infinitely many. Both interpretations are distinguished.
  • 8.4第61题按原页保留首个2√2后缺少y的式子；第62题实际退化为两条相交直线，不能按题组标题硬判双曲线。8.4#61 retains the printed expression with no y after its first 2√2. In#62 the equation degenerates to two intersecting lines, despite the exercise group’s hyperbola wording.
  • 8.4第82题的声音先后条件彼此矛盾，按印刷条件无实际位置；第87题的绝对距离差产生四个合法交点，全部保留。The sound-arrival conditions in 8.4#82 are inconsistent, so there is no physical position as printed. The absolute distance differences in#87 produce four valid intersections, all retained.
  • 8.5例1(c)原式表示空集，原文椭圆分类不成立。第8题未排除退化，不能仅由判别式断言双曲线。第79题原公式的根号分支需按B的符号补全。The original 8.5 Example 1(c) has an empty real locus, contradicting its printed ellipse classification. Exercise 8 does not exclude degeneracy. Exercise 79 needs the square-root branch to account for the sign of B.
  • 8.6保留负极径描出的双曲线两支。第45题未指明轨道轴的正方向，网页注明两种角度解释对应不同近日距离。Section 8.6 retains both hyperbola branches traced by signed radii. Exercise 45 does not direct the orbital axis; the two angle interpretations and their different closest distances are qualified.
  • 8.7第71题第二支区间原印π/2<t<2π/2，只覆盖半支；完整左支应为π/2<t<3π/2。第75、76题的极限端点与参数禁值均保留。In 8.7#71 the printed second interval π/2<t<2π/2 covers only half a branch; the full left branch requires π/2<t<3π/2. Exercises 75 and 76 retain limiting endpoints and excluded parameters.
  • 章末8.2复习例3的最后公式交换了变量位置；按顶点(−2,1)、焦点(−2,5)应为(x+2)²=16(y−1)。Test A第1题照印焦点(0,12)、准线x=−12，顶点为(−6,12)。The last formula in the 8.2 review Example 3 swaps coordinates. Vertex(−2,1) and focus(−2,5) give(x+2)²=16(y−1). Test A 1 retains focus(0,12) and directrix x=−12, giving vertex(−6,12).
  • 复习第78题的成本等式要求有符号距离差，只取双曲线右支。参数曲线的实际轨迹、运动方向、重复描绘次数与弹道物理时间区间均单独检查。Review 78’s cost equation requires a signed distance difference, so only the right hyperbola branch qualifies. Actual traces, orientation, repetitions and physical projectile time intervals are checked separately.
