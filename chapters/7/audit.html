原文差异与数学复审Source nuances and mathematical review

  • 7.1习题115没有给出各工作的工时；保留教材预期的时薪算术平均，并说明时间加权解释缺条件。7.1#115 does not specify hours worked. The expected arithmetic mean of hourly rates is retained, while a time-weighted reading is identified as underdetermined.
  • 7.1习题139和7.4习题99的参数分类包含零系数、矛盾零行及全零情形；不把退化方程当成普通直线。Parameter classifications in 7.1#139 and 7.4#99 include zero coefficients, contradictory zero rows, and the all-zero case rather than treating every equation as a genuine line.
  • 7.2的组织吸收区间有重叠，第57题A=0.28可以落入健康或肿瘤区间，按原表保留全部可能。The tissue-absorption intervals in 7.2 overlap. In#57,A=0.28 can be healthy or tumorous under the original table, so both possibilities remain.
  • 7.3习题97–98原文写具体距离但要求不等式；覆盖圆盘模型与精确距离的公共圆周点解释分开说明。7.3#97–98 state distances but request inequalities. Coverage-disk models are distinguished from the common-circle-boundary interpretation of exact distances.
  • 7.3习题125–126原页缺少开头的绝对值竖线。保留原图，并明确按标准绝对值读法给条件解析，未把补印当作原文完整无误。7.3#125–126 lack opening absolute-value bars in the original printing. The source crop is retained and the standard absolute-value reading is explicitly conditional.
  • 7.4习题75b原书称所示矩阵为最简阶梯形，但其中一项实际仅是阶梯形；保留原矩阵，按秩正确分类。7.4#75 bdescribes its displayed matrices as reduced echelon, although one is only echelon. The original matrix is retained and the solution count is determined by rank.
  • 7.7习题15的三个变量条件不相容。保留原矩阵，解释为什么前两个条件所得x=1,y=2不满足第三个条件。The three variable-entry conditions in 7.7#15 are inconsistent. The original matrices remain, with an explanation that x=1,y=2 from the first two conditions fails the third.
  • 7.7习题87的(b)原文在讨论N时仍写M，并把沿y轴移动写成左右移动；原文与互动缺源一并注明。7.7#87(b)mentions M while discussing N and says left/right along the y-axis. These source ambiguities are disclosed together with the unavailable applet.
  • 7.8投入产出引言的文字与所示矩阵的非对角项次序有差异；例题与配练明确采用原书显示的矩阵及AX展开，不偷偷替换模型。The 7.8 input-output introduction appears to swap the off-diagonal requirements between prose and the displayed matrix. Examples and practice explicitly use the original displayed matrix and AX expansion.
  • 章末复习第16题第三行照印为“2x+2y−7”，没有等号。保留原文；若解释为2x+2y=7则无解，但该解释与完整原题不同。Review#16 prints its third line as“2 x+2 y−7”with no equals sign. The original remains; interpreting it as 2 x+2 y=7 gives no solution, explicitly as a conditional correction.
  • 复习第79题区分正方形代数候选与“短边”条件；第81题利润按成本的15%计算，实际整件销量向上取整。Review#79 distinguishes the algebraic square candidate from the shorter-sides condition. Review#81 uses 15%of cost and rounds the required whole-unit sales upward.
